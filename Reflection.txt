1. Which type of emails were hardest to classify?
Emails with mixed intent (e.g., reminder + request, 
or informational emails with an implicit action) 
were the hardest to classify.

2. Why did your rules fail in some cases?
Rule-based logic failed when emails used different wording, 
indirect language, or overlapping keywords, 
which the if / elif / else conditions could not fully capture.

3. How could an LLM improve this process?
An LLM can understand context, intent, and semantics, 
allowing it to correctly classify emails even when phrasing varies or 
intent is implicit, reducing dependency on rigid rules.
